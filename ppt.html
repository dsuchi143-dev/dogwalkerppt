😤 The Problem

🔍

No Trusted Walker

Dog owners struggle to find verified, reliable walkers for their pets in their locality.

📅

No Easy Booking

No platform to schedule, track, and manage dog walking sessions conveniently.

💰

No Transparent Pricing

Hidden charges and no standardized pricing for professional dog walking services.

🔒

Safety Concerns

No background verification of walkers. Pet owners worried about their dog's safety.

✅ Our Solution

📱

Mobile App

One-tap booking for dog walks. Available on Android & iOS. Real-time walker tracking.

🦮

Verified Walkers

Aadhaar + PAN verified walkers. GPS tracked. Background checked for safety.

💳

Secure Payment

UPI, GPay, PhonePe, Paytm — all supported. Powered by Razorpay gateway.

⚡ Key Features

👤

User Registration

Email & phone OTP verification. Secure login with JWT tokens.

🐕

Dog Management

Add multiple dogs with breed, age, weight & special notes.

📅

Smart Booking

Select plan, dog, date & time. Auto date/time picker. Walker auto-assigned.

📍

GPS Tracking

Walker location verified during registration. Walk tracking for safety.

🔔

Notifications

Booking alerts, walker assignment, walk completion — all real-time.

🦮

Walker Dashboard

Separate walker app. Online/offline toggle. Accept/reject bookings.
